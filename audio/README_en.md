# Audio: music, sound effects, voices

What the videos sound like and how it was made, so you can produce something with the same feel. The detailed pages are in Chinese; the prompts for the narrator are in English as sent.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [music.md](music.md) | Background music and sound effects per episode: title, composer, source, ID. **The audio files themselves are not in this repository** (see below) |
| [tts.md](tts.md) | How the voices are made: the speech services and models for the narrator and each character, voice names, prompts verbatim, post-processing, pronunciation fixes, how takes are picked and checked |
| [tts/voices_breeze.json](tts/voices_breeze.json) | Voice table for all characters (character → voice ID, voice name, one-line persona) |
| [tts/ep10_tones.md](tts/ep10_tones.md) | Episode 10 per-line tone sheet, verbatim: the delivery direction sent with each line, and the few lines whose spoken text was rewritten |

## Why there are no audio files

- **Music and sound effects** come from [Mixkit](https://mixkit.co). Its license allows free use in videos (including commercial use) without attribution, but does not allow redistributing the assets themselves. So only the list is here; every track can be downloaded from the original site by its ID. A few sound effects were synthesized with ffmpeg, as noted in the list.
- **Voice recordings** are not included: they are generated by third-party speech services and the voices belong to those services. What is published is the voice names, prompts and parameters, which is enough to reproduce the same voices.

## In short

- Narrator: Gemini TTS, voice `Charon`, with a fixed style prompt plus an optional per-line direction.
- AI characters and the quartermaster: Breeze TTS 2 stock voices, instruction = persona + per-line tone, sped up 1.1× afterwards.
- Doubao: Volcano Engine `seed-tts-2.0`, voice `zh_female_shuangkuaisisi_uranus_bigtts`.
- Every toned line gets three takes; a Gemini model listens and picks one. Narration is also checked with local speech recognition for lines read twice or cut short.
